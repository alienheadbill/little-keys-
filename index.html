A family recording project

🎹 Little Keys Studio

Turn her first keyboard moments into music samples. Keep special family voices when they belong, and leave the original videos untouched.

1. Import a recording

WAV, MP3 and M4A recommended. MP4 video commonly cannot be decoded directly. On iPhone use Shortcuts: Select Photos → Encode Media (Audio Only) → Save File, then import the M4A. Files stay on your device.

Choose a recording to begin.

Video audio extraction (local, no upload). Keep this tab open while the video plays silently in the background.

2. Find her favorite notes

This first version does not automatically remove speech, quantize notes, or turn performances into MIDI. Use the sliders to avoid dialogue that covers important piano notes.

3. Loop workshop

Suggestions use changes in loudness, not AI transcription. Listen before deciding whether a clip includes speech.

    AI source separation

    Try a real MDX-Net vocal/instrumental separation model in your browser. First run downloads about 67 MB of model weights. Processing may be slow or exceed iPhone memory; desktop is more reliable. No recording is uploaded.

    Not processed.

    AI separation can distort notes or leave voice bleed. Compare with the original; preserve family moments as separate cuts. If your device cannot run the model, the desktop Demucs companion in the repository is an alternative.

    Voice activity and timing

    Optional Silero AI speech detection runs locally after downloading a model. It marks likely speech; it cannot separate voices from notes occupying the same moments.

      For clean piano with overlapping conversation, use desktop AI separation below; muting detected regions is not separation.

      Timing correction shifts detected attacks to nearby beat positions. It can change the feel of her original playing, so keep an untouched copy.

      The gate can reduce background noise during quiet passages. When talking overlaps piano notes, reliable voice separation needs a dedicated separation model; this app does not currently provide that.
